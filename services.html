FAQ

Common Questions About Our Services

❓ How long does the placement process take?+
📌 Our placement process typically takes 30-60 days depending on your profile, target role, and market conditions. We work diligently to get you placed as quickly as possible.
❓ What industries do you serve?+
📌 We serve IT, Finance, Healthcare, Manufacturing, FMCG, and more. Our strongest focus is on IT/Software roles across India and globally.
❓ Do you charge candidates?+
📌 We offer both free and paid service packages. Our basic profile review is free. Premium services like resume writing and coaching are paid. Contact us to know more.
❓ Can you help freshers?+
📌 Absolutely! We have dedicated career foundation programs for freshers including resume building, skill assessment, and connecting them with entry-level opportunities.
❓ Do you support payment plans?+
📌 Yes! For our premium packages, we require partial payment upfront and the rest upon placement. Contact us to discuss flexible payment options.
❓ What if I do not get placed?+
📌 We work with you until you get placed. Our complete package comes with unlimited support until you secure a role that matches your profile.
❓ Can you help with abroad placements?+
📌 Yes! We help with Gulf, Canada, USA, UK, and other international placements for eligible profiles with strong experience.
❓ Is my data safe with you?+
📌 Absolutely. We never share your personal data or resume without your explicit consent. Your privacy is our priority.
