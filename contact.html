📌 Our placement process typically takes 30-60 days depending on your profile, target role, and market conditions. We work diligently to get you placed as quickly as possible.
❓ What industries do you serve?+
📌 We serve IT, Finance, Healthcare, Manufacturing, FMCG, and more. Our strongest focus is on IT/Software roles across India and globally.

📌 Absolutely! We have dedicated career foundation programs for freshers including resume building, skill assessment, and connecting them with entry-level opportunities.
