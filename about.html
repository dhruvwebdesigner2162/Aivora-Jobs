Our Story

Bridging talent & opportunity since 2023

Aivora Jobs was founded with a simple yet powerful vision — to help talented professionals find the right opportunities and help companies find the right talent using the power of AI and smart matchmaking.

What started as a small team of HR professionals has grown into one of India's most trusted AI-powered placement consultancies. We've placed over 500 professionals across IT, Finance, Healthcare, and more.

Our approach is deeply personalized — we don't just send resumes; we build careers. Every candidate who joins our network gets dedicated attention from our expert team combined with cutting-edge AI tools.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does the placement process take?+
Our placement process typically takes 30–60 days depending on your profile, target role, and market conditions. We work diligently to get you placed as quickly as possible.
What industries do you serve?+
We serve IT, Finance, Healthcare, Manufacturing, FMCG, and more. Our strongest focus is on IT/Software roles across India and globally.
Can you help freshers?+
Absolutely! We have dedicated career foundation programs for freshers including resume building, skill assessment, and connecting them with entry-level opportunities.
How is Aivora Jobs different from other consultancies?+
We use AI-powered job matching to connect you with the right roles faster. We focus on quality over quantity and offer personalized attention unlike traditional consultancies.
Can you help with abroad placements?+
Yes! We help with Gulf, Canada, USA, UK, and other international placements for eligible profiles with strong experience.
Is my data safe with you?+
Absolutely. We never share your personal data or resume without your explicit consent. Your privacy is our priority.
